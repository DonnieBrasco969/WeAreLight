A Poem for You

Search the Light

"A thought on freedom, darkness, and what we choose to carry..."

Our souls are the source of loving,
Our conscience holds the inner control,
And when you feel the darkness winning,
Remember that you have a soul.

Though chaos splits the world in two,
And rules seem lost in endless night,
The pain shouldn't be our main view,
But proof we're made to search the Light.

The freedom given in our hands,
Can build a paradise or cage,
A gift of grace to all the lands,
Or just a tool of hurt and rage.

So do not let the shadow bind
The gentle warmth inside your chest;
For even in a world gone blind,
The searching soul will find its rest.

"No matter how dark the night gets, remember that light and love are choices we carry within ourselves."
